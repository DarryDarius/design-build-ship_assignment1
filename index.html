First Winter Club

25 versions of a landing page for international students facing their first Chicago winter. The story of how I got to the final one.

Batch 1 · Going wide (v01–v04)

Four directions from four different eras and voices: an official weather alert, an explorer's diary, a wordless assembly manual, and a Cold War pamphlet.

v01 · NWS AlertThe page as an official weather warning: alert banners, a teletype bulletin, and a live wind chill chart.
v02 · Expedition JournalA 1910s polar explorer's diary of a first winter, with a hand-drawn map of Hyde Park and a provisions list.
v03 · Assembly ManualFlat-pack instructions for "FÖRSTVINTER": almost no words, just a parts list and nine line-drawn steps.
v04 · Civil DefenseA 1950s two-color pamphlet: "Layer and Cover!", a warming-shelter sign, and a wind chill signal table.

Batch 2 · Interfaces and tones (v05–v08)

Same facts, four very different voices: data you check every morning, news you read, a rule you're told, and a letter from someone who's been through it.

v05 · Weather AppThe whole page is a forecast for your first winter: a huge "feels like −29°", a Nov–Mar outlook, and a notification-style join.
v06 · Newspaper"The Hyde Park Winter Gazette": a broadsheet front page with an engraved figure, gear-swap classifieds, and a clip-out coupon.
v07 · Brutalist Manifesto"Winter will not kill you. Ignorance will." Edge-to-edge type, eight numbered theses, one safety-orange accent.
v08 · A LetterA slow single-column letter from a third-year from Chennai to "you, arriving this fall", ending in a P.S. invitation.
