First Winter Club

25 versions of a landing page for international students facing their first Chicago winter. The story of how I got to the final one.

Batch 1 · Going wide (v01–v04)

Four directions from four different eras and voices: an official weather alert, an explorer's diary, a wordless assembly manual, and a Cold War pamphlet.

v01 · NWS AlertThe page as an official weather warning: alert banners, a teletype bulletin, and a live wind chill chart.
v02 · Expedition JournalA 1910s polar explorer's diary of a first winter, with a hand-drawn map of Hyde Park and a provisions list.
v03 · Assembly ManualFlat-pack instructions for "FÖRSTVINTER": almost no words, just a parts list and nine line-drawn steps.
v04 · Civil DefenseA 1950s two-color pamphlet: "Layer and Cover!", a warming-shelter sign, and a wind chill signal table.

Batch 2 · Interfaces and tones (v05–v08)

Same facts, four very different voices: data you check every morning, news you read, a rule you're told, and a letter from someone who's been through it.

v05 · Weather AppThe whole page is a forecast for your first winter: a huge "feels like −29°", a Nov–Mar outlook, and a notification-style join.
v06 · Newspaper"The Hyde Park Winter Gazette": a broadsheet front page with an engraved figure, gear-swap classifieds, and a clip-out coupon.
v07 · Brutalist Manifesto"Winter will not kill you. Ignorance will." Edge-to-edge type, eight numbered theses, one safety-orange accent.
v08 · A LetterA slow single-column letter from a third-year from Chennai to "you, arriving this fall", ending in a P.S. invitation.

Batch 3 · Internet culture and systems (v09–v12)

The last wide batch: two nostalgic internet forms, and two ways of organizing winter as a system you can navigate.

v09 · GeoCities 1999Marquees, a visitor counter, an "under construction" banner, cursor-chasing snowflakes, and a "Sign my Guestbook!!" join.
v10 · Terminal$ winter --survive --first-time, a man page, and a live shell where you can run windchill or type join.
v11 · Gear CatalogA premium outdoor-catalog lookbook: exploded layering diagram, spec cards with "get it cheap", and a $0.00 order form.
v12 · Transit MapA departures board counting down to Thursday 6 PM, and four winter "lines" that all meet at the First Winter Club interchange.

Narrowing · Choosing a direction

After twelve directions I compared two ways forward: a bold "winter gazette" (v06 + v07 + v12) and a warm "gear guide" (v11 + v08 + v05). I chose the gear guide. A nervous first-winter student needs reassurance and a list they can act on, not a page that shows off. I dropped GeoCities and the terminal (too noisy, too technical), civil defense and the manifesto (too alarming), and the newspaper (too dense on a phone).

Batch 4 · Three ways to combine (v13–v15)

The same ingredients (Meera's voice from v08, the catalog cards from v11, the wind chill tool from v05/v01) arranged three ways.

v13 · Catalog Firstv11's catalog, now "Meera's copy": her note opens it, and her handwriting fills the margins of the product cards.
v14 · Letter Firstv08's letter, with spec cards pinned in wherever she mentions gear, and a fill-in-the-blank "feels like" widget.
v15 · Side by SideHer letter on the left, the catalog on the right. Scrolling the facts highlights the matching part of her story.
