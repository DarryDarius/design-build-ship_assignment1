# First Winter Club — AGENTS.md

## The idea
A landing page for **First Winter Club**, a peer support group that helps international students get through their first Chicago winter.

- **Who it's for:** UChicago international students facing their first Chicago winter, especially those from warm places who have never lived through snow or a −20°F wind chill.
- **What a visitor should understand:** Chicago winter is survivable if you prepare. Wind chill matters more than temperature, layering beats one big coat, and there are indoor routes and cheap places to buy gear.
- **What a visitor should do:** Join the club and get the free First Winter checklist.
