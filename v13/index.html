Before you shop — a note from Meera

Hi. I grew up in Chennai, where January means about 84°F and the warmest thing I owned was a school cardigan. Three winters ago nobody handed me a list, so I’ve gone through the club’s catalog with a pen.

Two things before you buy anything. Check the feels-like number, not the temperature: my first really cold morning, my phone said 2°F and the lake wind made it feel like −20°F. And you don’t need to spend much. Most of what I wear came from a thrift store, Uniqlo, a friend’s Costco run, or the November gear swap.

Skim the print. Read the scribbles: they’re mistakes I already made.

Meera

third year · still from Chennai · now also a little from here

The Layering System

Three thin layers beat one big coat.

pp. 02–03
Fig. 1 · Exploded view, inside → out
01

Base layer next to skin

A snug top and leggings that pull sweat off your skin. Never cotton: it stays wet.

02

Mid layer the warmth

Fleece or light down. It holds warm air around you.

03

Shell the weather

A windproof, waterproof, hooded parka. This is what stops the lake wind.

My first January: thickest cotton hoodie, one enormous coat. I sweated walking to my nine o’clock and froze walking home. Three thin things. Take one off indoors.— M.
