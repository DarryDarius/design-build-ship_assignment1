First Winter Club

a letter, for your first Chicago winter

Dear you, new here this fall,

If January at home means 84°F, like in Chennai, I know the winter you’re about to have.

My half of this page is my mistakes, so you can skip them. The short version: it’s survivable if you prepare.

→ 01 · Layers

I made the cotton hoodie mistake, which everyone from somewhere warm makes once: a thick hoodie under the heavy coat I’d packed from home. I sweated on the walk to my 9 AM, then shivered in damp cotton all afternoon.

Three thin layers fixed it. A base layer was the first thing I bought.

→ 02 · The Kit

The small things matter more than I expected: a hat over your ears, mittens so your fingers keep each other company, and boots with grip. I fell twice on the ice in sneakers before I gave in.

→ 03 · Rated To

My first really cold morning, my phone said 2°F. Then the wind came off the lake, and the number that mattered was the small one underneath: feels like −20°F. I wrote this on a sticky note for my window:

feels like −18°Ffrostbite in ~30 min

feels like −35°Fabout 10 min

feels like −48°Fabout 5 min

Now I check feels-like before I leave, like checking the sky for rain at home.

→ 04 · The Gear Swap

You don’t need to spend much. My parka was free, off the rack at the November gear swap. Half of what I wear once kept someone else warm.

And in December the sun sets around 4:20. Get outside at midday, and let people check in on you.

The rest is at the bottom. ↓

THU6 PMON CAMPUS

Thursday nights got me through. My first November, a girl from Lagos in my lab brought me to First Winter Club: a room on campus, a kettle, a pile of mismatched mittens, and students from Manila, São Paulo and Jakarta comparing their coldest days.

Nobody made me talk. Mostly we sat by the radiator, warm together, which turned out to be the point.
