Preparedness Actions

  1. Base layer: thermal top and leggings (merino or synthetic, never cotton).
  2. Mid layer: fleece or light down to trap heat.
  3. Shell: a windproof, waterproof parka that covers your hips.
  4. Extremities: hat over the ears, mittens beat gloves, scarf or neck gaiter over the face.
  5. Feet: insulated waterproof boots with real grip. Ice season lasts months.
  6. Light: sunset is around 4:20 PM in December. Get daylight at lunch and take vitamin D.
