Hi!!! WELCOME 2 our little corner of the World Wide Web!! If u just moved to Chicago from somewhere WARM (Mumbai? Lagos? Singapore? São Paulo??) and u r scared of ur first winter... DON'T PANIC. Chicago winter is 100% survivable if u prepare. Every1 in this club did it. Here is EVERYTHING we wish some1 told us.

❄︎ TIP #1: WIND CHILL IS THE REAL TEMP!!!

Ok so ur phone says 20°F. U think "not bad." WRONG!!! Add a 30 mph wind off the lake and it FEELS LIKE ~1°F on ur face. The wind steals heat from ur skin way faster. ALWAYS check the "feels like" number, not the temperature.

In January the wind chill goes below 0°F regularly and can hit −30°F or lower. During the Jan 2019 polar vortex the air was −23°F and the wind chill was near −50°F. (the city basically shut down. stay inside on days like that!!)

❄︎ TIP #2: 3 LAYERS > 1 BIG COAT

One giant puffy coat over a T-shirt = sweaty on the bus, frozen at the bus stop. LAYERS trap warm air AND u can take one off inside!!

  • BASE layer: thermal top + leggings, merino wool or synthetic. NEVER COTTON!!! cotton gets wet + stays cold.
  • MID layer: fleece or a light down jacket. this is the cozy part.
  • SHELL: windproof + waterproof parka that goes past ur hips.

❄︎ TIP #4: THE SUN SETS AT 4:20 PM?!?!

Yes. On Dec 21 Chicago gets about 9 hours 8 minutes of daylight and sunset is around 4:20 PM. It can mess with ur mood big time. What helps:

  • Go outside at midday when there IS light. even 15 min. even if cold.
  • Ask a doctor about vitamin D.
  • Check in on ur friends, and let them check on u. That's literally why this club exists.
