What every new arrival must know when the Chicago cold comes. Read it now. Know what to do. Your first winter is survivable if you are prepared.

The Three Layers
1

Base

Thermal top and leggings against the skin. Merino or synthetic. Cotton holds sweat and steals heat.

2

Middle

A fleece or light down jacket. This is the layer that traps the warmth your body makes.

3

Shell

A windproof, waterproof parka that reaches past the hips. The lake wind is the real enemy.

Wind ChillWhat It Means
+20°FCold. Hat and gloves. Walk briskly.
0°FAll three layers. Cover your ears and neck.
−18°FFrostbite on bare skin in about 30 minutes.
−35°FFrostbite in about 10 minutes. Cover your face. Go indoors.

Know the Other Danger: The Dark

  • In December the sun sets around 4:20 PM.
  • Get outside at midday when you can.
  • Take vitamin D. Ask a doctor.
  • Don't stay alone in your room for days. Check in with friends.
